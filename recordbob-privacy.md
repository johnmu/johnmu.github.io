**Effective date: August 30, 2026**

RecordBob is a manual measurement and climbing-grade tracker. The app does not
include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

## Data you add

RecordBob lets you save profile names, optional profile photos and notes, stat
definitions, recorded numeric values or climbing grades, dates, notes, and
progress history. This data is stored on your device and may sync through your
personal iCloud account when iCloud is available.

RecordBob does not transmit this data to a developer-operated server. The
developer does not sell or share it.

## Photos

Photos you choose for profiles stay in the app's data and may sync through your
personal iCloud account. RecordBob does not upload those photos to a
developer-operated server.

## Camera and photo library

RecordBob requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add or
change a profile photo. You can use the rest of the app without granting either
permission.

## Deleting data

You can delete individual entries from Log or Progress. You can delete a profile
or stat from Library; RecordBob warns that this also deletes the recorded entries
associated with that profile or stat. Deletions may sync to your other devices
through iCloud.

## Sharing

RecordBob sends no diagnostics automatically. If you choose **Share Support
Details**, iOS presents its standard share sheet and sends the app version,
device type, and iOS version only to the destination you select. The privacy
practices of that destination apply after you share.

## Children's privacy

The developer does not receive or have remote access to personal information
entered in RecordBob, including information about children. That information
remains on the user's device and may sync through the user's private iCloud
account.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective
date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit
[umnhoj.com/contact](https://umnhoj.com/contact/).
